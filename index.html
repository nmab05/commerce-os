Inventory reconciliation

  
Existing inventory can be built from photos, manual additions, selling-app imports, and uploaded inventory lists. Imports are reconciled first; nothing overwrites existing inventory automatically. Field-level differences require an explicit keep/use choice. Future marketplace connectors can also bring listing photos and platform IDs into the same matching engine.







  
Pricing model

  
“Pricing” is the product/listing price field. “Item Cost” is what you paid for the item. Platform-specific pricing can still differ by marketplace.
