Integrated Item Research

One workspace for item identity, photos, sold/active comps, alternate valuation sources, listing optimization, pricing rationale, and approval. This is the Phase 4 integrated research and approval workflow.
Use the item title/brand/category to launch public research. Add only relevant comps; Commerce OS calculates the range and price recommendation from the evidence you record.

Automated Market Research

One action builds exact-item searches from title, brand, category and photo, attempts browser-accessible evidence collection, scores the evidence, and calculates a market valuation. Sources the browser cannot read automatically are clearly marked for browser-assisted review rather than treated as researched.

Phase 5 decision gate

Research → Recommendation → User approval → Vendoo execution → Verification. Market value is calculated independently of acquisition cost. Verified acquisition cost is used only for profitability.
